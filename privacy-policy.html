Accessible Blind Cafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

App: Accessible Blind Cafe (Android package com.pvskhalsa.app.blindcafe)

Developer: PVS Khalsa

Accessible Blind Cafe is a directory of groups, channels and contacts for the blind community. It helps blind users find communities, reach them easily and collaborate with each other. This page explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code in the app.
  • On first run, the app shows a welcome screen with the app's name, the developer's name and the app's purpose, asks you to agree to this privacy policy (the box starts unticked), and asks your age group. Your age choice is stored on your phone and is not sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • The app downloads all published directory entries (names, descriptions, audience category and contact links) from the developer's Google Firebase Firestore database over the internet, and keeps a copy on your phone so the directory loads fast.
  • Owners can send their group or channel details to the developer with the "Register a group or channel" form, only after agreeing to the directory rules and confirming that the owner agreed to publish those details.
  • Announcements arrive by push notification only if you allow notifications. Announcements are sent to topic channels using Firebase Cloud Messaging; the app does not upload your device token to the developer's database, and the app admin never sees it. Google's privacy policy covers how Google handles the token: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • The app uses Google's Play In-App Update library to check whether an update is available and offer it. This connects to Google Play, and Google's privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Your choices (age group, favorites, text size, theme and your notifications list) are stored on your phone. Android automatic backup is disabled; device-maker transfer tools may behave differently.
  • Contact links (for example WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, phone or email) open those apps or sites. They are separate services with their own privacy policies.

Age-appropriate content

  • The app is designed for users aged 13 and older. It is not intended for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • When you set up the app, you choose your age group. If you choose 18 or older, the directory shows all published entries. If you choose under 18, the app hides entries marked for adults and entries with no audience set, so the directory shows only entries marked "Under 18" or "Both". This filtering happens on your phone: the app downloads the full published directory to the device and then filters what it displays.
  • Owners who register a group or channel must say whether its content is for adults (18 or older), for under 18s, or for both. People aged 13 to 17 can register groups or channels as well; their submissions contain the same details (including owner name, email and mobile number when shared) as any other submission and are handled the same way.

Child safety

  • Accessible Blind Cafe does not allow content that sexually abuses or exploits children, or any behaviour that endangers children. Our full Child Safety Standards are published at https://pvskhalsa.github.io/blindcafe/child-safety.html.
  • You can report a safety concern from the app's menu. The report opens a prepared email to our safety contact on your device; you choose whether to send it.
  • The developer reads every safety report and takes action on confirmed violations, including removing listings that break these rules. Where the law requires it, we report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities as soon as we know of it or have reason to suspect it - we do not wait to confirm it ourselves first.

The directory

  • The app downloads the published directory entries - each entry's name, platform, type, link, category, language, country, audience category, description, and the owner's name, email and mobile number when the owner chose to share them - and keeps a copy on your phone. Entries marked as pending or removed are not downloaded.

Register a group or channel

  • With the Register form, an owner (or someone acting for them) can submit a group or channel for the directory. Before the form opens, the app shows the directory rules and regulations and asks the submitter to agree to them.
  • The app sends the details typed into the form - name, platform, type, link, category, language, country, audience category, owner name, email, mobile number and description - together with the submission time and a record that the consent box was ticked, to the developer's Firestore database as a pending submission. The consent checkbox is off by default; the submission is sent only after the submitter ticks it to confirm the owner agreed to publish these details. The developer reviews pending submissions and publishes approved ones; published entries, including the owner's shared contact details and audience category, become visible to app users (subject to age filtering).

Service providers

  • The directory database and announcement delivery run on Google Firebase: Cloud Firestore stores the directory and pending submissions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers announcements to your phone. Update checks use Google Play's In-App Update library. Google's own privacy policy covers how Google processes data on these services: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What stays on your phone

  • The app saves your choices on your phone: your chosen age group, your favorite entries, the text size and theme you choose, the downloaded directory copy, and the list of announcements you received. It does not send these anywhere. The Android setting "Clear storage" deletes them. This page does not cover copies a phone maker's own transfer tool may make.

Permissions

  • The app declares exactly two permissions: INTERNET and POST_NOTIFICATIONS. It does not declare contacts, SMS, location, microphone, camera, call log or any other permission.
  • Internet: downloads the directory, sends register-form submissions, connects to Firebase Cloud Messaging so announcements can arrive, and checks for app updates.
  • Notifications: shows announcements. You can refuse this permission and still use the directory.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

  • For privacy questions, or to ask the developer to correct or remove a directory entry or personal details, use the "Report a safety concern" option in the app's menu: it opens a prepared email to the developer's contact address on your device, and you choose whether to send it. You can also reach the developer, PVS Khalsa, using the contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
