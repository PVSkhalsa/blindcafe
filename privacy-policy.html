Accessible Blind Cafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

App: Accessible Blind Cafe (Android package com.pvskhalsa.app.blindcafe)

Developer: PVS Khalsa

Accessible Blind Cafe is a directory of groups, channels and contacts for the blind community. It helps blind users find communities, reach them easily and collaborate with each other. This page explains what the app does with information.

Summary

  • The version of the app now on Google Play (26.11.28) requires sign-in to use the app: everyone signs in with their Google account; invited team members may instead sign in with email and password. An upcoming update (26.11.29) has no accounts and no sign-in: the directory works without any personal details. This policy describes both versions: the sections that mention sign-in, accounts, personal notifications and account deletion apply to version 26.11.28 and to devices that have not updated yet. After your device updates to 26.11.29, the app no longer collects or stores that account data, but any account data already stored under 26.11.28 stays covered by those sections until the developer finishes deleting it, and you can still ask for your stored data to be deleted (see Contact) until then. The developer will delete the stored ordinary-user account data described here as part of that change; the separate web-administration accounts of the developer's team are outside its scope. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code in the app.
  • On first run, the app shows a welcome screen with the app's name, the developer's name and the app's purpose, asks you to agree to this privacy policy (the box starts unticked), and asks your age group (version 26.11.28) or your birth year (version 26.11.29). Your answer is stored on your phone and is not sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • The app downloads all published directory entries (names, descriptions, audience category and contact links) from the developer's Google Firebase Firestore database over the internet, and keeps a copy on your phone so the directory loads fast.
  • Owners can send their group or channel details to the developer with the "Register a group or channel" form, only after agreeing to the directory rules and confirming that the owner agreed to publish those details.
  • Announcements arrive by push notification only if you allow notifications. Announcements to everyone are sent to a topic channel using Firebase Cloud Messaging; the app does not upload your device token to the developer for these, though Google processes the token on its servers to deliver the broadcasts. In version 26.11.28, if you sign in, the app stores your device's push token with your account so the owner can send you personal notifications and in-app messages, and removes it when you sign out or delete your account. Version 26.11.29 uses only the topic announcements: it does not register or upload any new token to the developer. Tokens already stored with 26.11.28 accounts remain covered by the Your account section until the developer finishes deleting them. Google's privacy policy covers how Google handles tokens: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • The app uses Google's Play In-App Update library to check whether an update is available and offer it. This connects to Google Play, and Google's privacy policy applies to that connection.
  • Your choices (age group or birth year, favorites, text size, theme and your notifications list) are stored on your phone. Android automatic backup is disabled; device-maker transfer tools may behave differently. In version 26.11.28, if you sign in, your account also stores your name, email address, sign-in provider, account creation time and your notification inbox in the developer's Firebase project.
  • Contact links (for example WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, phone or email) open those apps or sites. They are separate services with their own privacy policies.

Age-appropriate content

  • The app is designed for users aged 13 and older. It is not intended for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • When you set up the app, you choose your age group (version 26.11.28) or enter your birth year (version 26.11.29; the year alone, stored only on your phone). Version 26.11.29 applies conservative boundaries based on the calendar-year difference, not your exact age: the current calendar year minus your birth year must be at least 14 to set up the app, and at least 19 for the adult band. A year close to either boundary is resolved conservatively (a borderline year near the lower limit is not accepted, and a borderline year near 19 is treated as under 18). An age group saved by an earlier version is kept. In the adult band, the directory shows all published entries. If you are under 18, the app hides entries marked for adults and entries with no audience set, so the directory shows only entries marked "Under 18" or "Both". This filtering happens on your phone: the app downloads the full published directory to the device and then filters what it displays.
  • Owners who register a group or channel must say whether its content is for adults (18 or older), for under 18s, or for both. People aged 13 to 17 can register groups or channels as well; their submissions contain the same details (including owner name, email and mobile number when shared) as any other submission and are handled the same way.

Child safety

  • Accessible Blind Cafe does not allow content that sexually abuses or exploits children, or any behaviour that endangers children. Our full Child Safety Standards are published at https://pvskhalsa.github.io/blindcafe/child-safety.html.
  • You can report a safety concern from the app's menu. The report opens a prepared email to our safety contact on your device; you choose whether to send it.
  • The developer reads every safety report and takes action on confirmed violations, including removing listings that break these rules. Where the law requires it, we report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities as soon as we know of it or have reason to suspect it - we do not wait to confirm it ourselves first.

The directory

  • The app downloads the published directory entries - each entry's name, platform, type, link, category, language, country, audience category, description, and the owner's name, email and mobile number when the owner chose to share them - and keeps a copy on your phone. Entries marked as pending or removed are not downloaded.

Register a group or channel

  • With the Register form, an owner (or someone acting for them) can submit a group or channel for the directory. Before the form opens, the app shows the directory rules and regulations and asks the submitter to agree to them.
  • The app sends the details typed into the form - name, platform, type, link, category, language, country, audience category, owner name, email, mobile number and description - together with the submission time and a record that the consent box was ticked, to the developer's Firestore database as a pending submission. The consent checkbox is off by default; the submission is sent only after the submitter ticks it to confirm the owner agreed to publish these details. The developer reviews pending submissions and publishes approved ones; published entries, including the owner's shared contact details and audience category, become visible to app users (subject to age filtering).

Your account

  • This section describes version 26.11.28 (the version now on Google Play), which requires sign-in. Version 26.11.29 removes accounts entirely: there is no sign-in, and no new account data is collected. Any account data already stored under 26.11.28 remains covered by this section until the developer completes the deletion below, and the deletion routes in this section stay available to former users until then. The developer will delete the stored ordinary-user accounts, notification inboxes, push tokens and login accounts described here as part of that change. This deletion covers ordinary user accounts only; the separate accounts the developer's team uses for web administration are outside its scope and are not deleted by it. When you sign in, the app stores your name, email address, sign-in provider (Google, or email for team members) and account creation time with your account in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • You can change your display name in the app's Account tab. A name can be changed at most once per month; this limit is enforced by the server, not just the app.
  • If you are signed in, the app keeps your received personal notifications in an inbox attached to your account. Only you and the developer can read it.
  • You can sign out at any time; signing out removes this device's push token from your account.
  • You can delete your account and its stored details at any time in the app's Account tab. Deletion removes your profile, this device's push token and your sign-in (login account) step by step; if a deletion is interrupted (for example the connection drops), it completes automatically on your next sign-in. Remaining records attached to the deleted account (such as your notification inbox) are removed by the developer. The owner can also delete an account from the admin panel for moderation; that removes the same data and the login account. Deletion does not remove an active suspension or ban record (see Moderation records below). You can also ask the developer to delete your account without using the app, using the contact route in the Contact section below.
  • Moderation records: if the owner suspends or bans your account, the app stores the reason and (for a suspension) its end time, plus when it was placed. The reason is shown to you in the app while the suspension or ban is in effect. A suspension or ban record keeps only your account id, the reason, and when it was placed; it is kept for as long as the suspension or ban stays in effect, even if the rest of your account data is deleted, so a suspension or ban cannot be bypassed by deleting and recreating a profile. Lifting the suspension or unbanning removes the record.

Service providers

  • The app runs on Google Firebase: Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone and Cloud Firestore stores the directory and pending submissions. In version 26.11.28, Firebase Authentication also handles sign-in and Cloud Firestore also stores accounts and notification inboxes. Version 26.11.29 still uses Cloud Firestore for the directory and pending submissions, but does not use Firebase Authentication and stores no accounts or notification inboxes. Update checks use Google Play's In-App Update library. Access to stored data is enforced by server-side security rules: only you can read your account, tokens and inbox; the developer can read accounts only for support and deletion requests. Google's own privacy policy covers how Google processes data on these services: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What stays on your phone

  • The app saves your choices on your phone: your chosen age group (version 26.11.28) or your birth year and the age band derived from it (version 26.11.29), your favorite entries, the text size and theme you choose, the downloaded directory copy, and the list of announcements you received. It does not send these anywhere. The Android setting "Clear storage" deletes them. In version 26.11.28, your account also stores your notification inbox in the developer's Firebase project. This page does not cover copies a phone maker's own transfer tool may make.

Permissions

  • The app declares exactly two permissions: INTERNET and POST_NOTIFICATIONS. It does not declare contacts, SMS, location, microphone, camera, call log or any other permission.
  • Internet: downloads the directory, sends register-form submissions, connects to Firebase Cloud Messaging so announcements can arrive, and checks for app updates.
  • Notifications: shows announcements. You can refuse this permission and still use the directory.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

  • For privacy questions, or to ask the developer to correct or remove a directory entry or personal details, use the "Report a safety concern" option in the app's menu: it opens a prepared email to the developer's contact address on your device, and you choose whether to send it. You can also reach the developer, PVS Khalsa, using the contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing - this route works without the app and can be used to request account deletion.
