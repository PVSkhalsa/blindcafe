Child Safety Standards

Accessible Blind Cafe does not allow content that sexually abuses or exploits children, or any behaviour that endangers children.

  • Every new public listing is reviewed by the app owner before it becomes visible.
  • Users can report a safety concern from the app's menu. The report opens a prepared email to our safety contact; the user sends it themselves.
  • The owner reads every safety report and takes action on confirmed violations, including removing listings that break these rules.
  • Where the law requires it, confirmed child sexual abuse material is reported to the appropriate authorities.
